Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for students and parents.

Does the enquiry form confirm my room?

No. It only sends us your request. Your room is confirmed after you visit, see the room and we verify availability.

Can parents visit before booking?

Yes, parents and students are welcome to visit. Please call or message us first so we can show you around.

What is included in the monthly fee?

Exact inclusions and the deposit are shared with you during your visit.

Are boys and girls accommodated separately?

Yes, boys and girls have separate arrangements. Details such as blocks, entry rules and visiting hours are explained when you visit.

What documents are needed to join?

We will tell you which documents are needed when you enquire or visit.
